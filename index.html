Our Story

Made by hand,
the slow way

Abercrombie & Scents began at a kitchen counter — a single pot of goat milk soap poured for family, then friends, then anyone who asked. What started as a small ritual grew into a craft we take seriously.

Every bar and every bottle is still made in small batches by hand, with real botanicals and nothing you can't pronounce. Tell us the scents and herbs you love, and we'll blend them just for you.

— The Abercrombie & Scents Family
